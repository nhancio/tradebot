# Social Media Strategy — TradeBot

## 1. Product Name & Category
**Product:** TradeBot
**Category:** AI-powered Trading Bot & Analytics Platform (Fintech/AI)

## 2. Target Audience
**Who are we selling to?**
- Crypto and stock traders (retail).
- Investment enthusiasts looking for automated strategies.
- Tech-savvy individuals interested in algorithmic trading.

## 3. Primary Distribution Channels
- **Twitter/X:** The primary hub for real-time market discussion and "Crypto Twitter."
- **Discord:** For building a community of traders and sharing live signals.
- **Telegram:** Direct communication and automated alert channels.
- **YouTube:** In-depth strategy breakdowns and platform tutorials.

## 4. Content Strategy
- **Live Trading Results:** (Transparent) screenshots of bot performance and signals.
- **AI Market Analysis:** Daily breakdowns of market trends analyzed by TradeBot.
- **Strategy Deep Dives:** Educational content on how the underlying algorithms work.
- **Risk Management Tips:** Positioning the tool as a way to trade "smarter, not harder."

## 5. Growth Hack / Viral Loop
**The "Refer a Trader" Loop:**
Implement a tiered referral program where users get a percentage of the trading fees (or subscription cost) for every trader they bring to the platform. Additionally, offer "Free Signal Alerts" to anyone who joins the public Discord/Telegram, acting as a lead magnet.
